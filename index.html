What do the strength levels mean?

Your badge compares your best set with lifters at your selected gender and body weight. The time ranges below describe typical experience; training years alone do not determine your badge.

★BeginnerAt least 1 monthCan perform the movement correctly · stronger than 5% of lifters
★NoviceAt least 6 monthsRegular practice of the technique · stronger than 20%
★IntermediateAt least 2 yearsRegular practice of the technique · stronger than 50%
★AdvancedOver 5 yearsSteady progress with the lift · stronger than 80%
★EliteOver 5 yearsDedicated training toward strength-sport competition · stronger than 95%

Percentiles and experience descriptions: StrengthLevel standards.
